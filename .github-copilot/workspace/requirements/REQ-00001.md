# REQ-00001: Quan ly Nguoi dung va Giao dien (User Auth & UI)

## Metadata
- **Brief nguon**: BRIEF-00001.md
- **Ngay tao**: 2026-03-27
- **Trang thai**: DRAFT - cho PO phe duyet

## Tom tat
Tai lieu nay dac ta yeu cau cho module Quan ly Nguoi dung va Giao dien nen tang cua he thong Team Kanban, bao gom dang ky, dang nhap, dang xuat, cap nhat profile co ban va bo khung giao dien responsive.

## Actors
- **Khach (Guest)**: Nguoi chua dang nhap, co the dang ky va dang nhap.
- **Nguoi dung da dang nhap (Authenticated User)**: Quan ly profile va dang xuat.
- **He thong**: Xu ly xac thuc, quan ly session, kiem tra hop le du lieu va render giao dien.

## Yeu cau Chuc nang

### FR-00001-01: Dang ky tai khoan
**Mo ta**: Guest co the tao tai khoan moi bang thong tin hop le.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Given Guest dang o trang dang ky
  When Guest nhap day du thong tin bat buoc hop le va gui form
  Then he thong tao tai khoan moi thanh cong va thong bao ket qua cho nguoi dung
- Given Guest dang o trang dang ky
  When Guest gui form voi email hoac username da ton tai
  Then he thong tu choi dang ky va hien thong bao loi ro rang
- Given Guest dang o trang dang ky
  When Guest gui form thieu truong bat buoc hoac sai dinh dang
  Then he thong khong tao tai khoan va hien loi validate tuong ung

### FR-00001-02: Dang nhap he thong
**Mo ta**: Guest co the dang nhap bang thong tin tai khoan hop le.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Given Guest dang o trang dang nhap va tai khoan ton tai
  When Guest nhap dung username hoac email cung voi mat khau va gui form
  Then he thong dang nhap thanh cong, tao session va chuyen den khu vuc sau dang nhap
- Given Guest dang o trang dang nhap
  When Guest nhap sai mat khau hoac username/email khong ton tai
  Then he thong tu choi dang nhap va hien thong bao loi phu hop

### FR-00001-03: Dang xuat he thong
**Mo ta**: Nguoi dung da dang nhap co the ket thuc phien lam viec an toan.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Given Nguoi dung dang dang nhap
  When Nguoi dung thuc hien hanh dong dang xuat
  Then he thong huy session hien tai va chuyen ve trang dang nhap hoac trang chu cong khai

### FR-00001-04: Quan ly profile ca nhan
**Mo ta**: Nguoi dung da dang nhap co the xem va cap nhat thong tin profile.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Given Nguoi dung dang dang nhap
  When Nguoi dung truy cap trang profile
  Then he thong hien thi thong tin profile gom username va mat khau (duoi dang an)
- Given Nguoi dung dang dang nhap
  When Nguoi dung cap nhat username hoac mat khau voi du lieu hop le
  Then he thong luu thay doi thanh cong va hien thong bao cap nhat thanh cong
- Given Nguoi dung dang dang nhap
  When Nguoi dung cap nhat username hoac mat khau voi du lieu khong hop le
  Then he thong tu choi luu va hien loi validate tuong ung

### FR-00001-05: Khong ho tro anh dai dien
**Mo ta**: Trong pham vi module nay, he thong khong ho tro tai len hoac cap nhat anh dai dien.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Given Nguoi dung dang dang nhap va o trang profile
  When Nguoi dung truy cap khu vuc profile
  Then he thong khong hien thi chuc nang upload anh dai dien
- Given Nguoi dung co gang goi API hoac gui request cap nhat anh dai dien
  When request duoc gui len he thong
  Then he thong tu choi request voi thong bao chuc nang khong duoc ho tro trong pham vi hien tai

### FR-00001-06: Form validation client va server
**Mo ta**: Tat ca form lien quan auth/profile phai duoc kiem tra hop le o ca client va server.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Given Nguoi dung tuong tac voi form dang ky, dang nhap hoac profile
  When du lieu khong hop le theo quy tac da dinh nghia
  Then he thong chan submit khong hop le o client va van xac thuc lai o server truoc khi ghi du lieu
- Given Du lieu gui len server
  When du lieu khong hop le
  Then he thong tra ve thong bao loi theo tung truong bi sai
- Given Nguoi dung nhap username hoac mat khau
  When du lieu duoc validate
  Then he thong chi validate 2 truong username va mat khau trong pham vi module nay
- Given Nguoi dung nhap mat khau
  When mat khau ngan hon 8 ky tu hoac khong co dong thoi chu hoa va chu thuong
  Then he thong tu choi va hien loi mat khau khong dat chinh sach

### FR-00001-07: Giao dien nen tang va responsive
**Mo ta**: He thong cung cap layout tong the va cac trang co ban de lam nen tang cho cac module tiep theo.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Given Nguoi dung truy cap cac trang nen tang (trang chu, dang nhap, dang ky, profile)
  When trang duoc tai
  Then giao dien hien thi day du cac thanh phan chinh (layout, header, footer, navigation) theo thiet ke thong nhat
- Given Nguoi dung su dung thiet bi di dong hoac man hinh nho
  When truy cap he thong
  Then giao dien tuong thich responsive, khong vo bo cuc va cac thao tac chinh van su dung duoc
- Given Nguoi dung su dung desktop
  When truy cap he thong
  Then giao dien phai hien thi day du va thao tac on dinh tren desktop

### FR-00001-08: Phan quyen truy cap co ban
**Mo ta**: Khu vuc yeu cau xac thuc chi cho phep nguoi dung da dang nhap truy cap.

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- Given Guest chua dang nhap
  When Guest truy cap trang yeu cau dang nhap (vi du: profile)
  Then he thong chuyen huong Guest den trang dang nhap
- Given Nguoi dung da dang nhap
  When truy cap trang duoc cap quyen
  Then he thong cho phep truy cap va hien thi noi dung tuong ung

Quyet dinh pham vi quyen: module nay chi ap dung 2 vai tro Guest va Authenticated User, khong mo rong them vai tro khac.

## Yeu cau Phi chuc nang (NFR)
- **Tuong thich ky thuat**: Trien khai tren PHP 8.x, MySQL 8.x, HTML5, CSS3; han che framework khong can thiet (cho phep Bootstrap neu can).
- **Bao mat**: Mat khau phai duoc ma hoa truoc khi luu trong co so du lieu; session phai duoc quan ly de tranh su dung trai phep.
- **Kha dung**: Form va thong bao loi phai ro rang de nguoi dung co the sua loi nhanh.
- **Responsive**: Giao dien ho tro man hinh desktop va mobile cho cac trang nen tang.
- **Tien do**: Module nay la nen tang cho cac module khac nen duoc uu tien hoan thanh som.

## Constraints
- **Cong nghe**: PHP 8.x, MySQL 8.x, HTML5, CSS3.
- **Nghiep vu mon hoc**: Dap ung yeu cau co CRUD va form validation theo CT214H.
- **Phu thuoc**: Cac module quan ly project/task phu thuoc vao bo giao dien va xac thuc cua module nay.

## Out of Scope
- Chuc nang quen mat khau/lay lai mat khau qua OTP email.
- Dang nhap bang tai khoan mang xa hoi (Google, Facebook, ...).
- Xoa tai khoan vinh vien do nguoi dung tu thuc hien.
- Upload va quan ly anh dai dien.
