\section{Design Principles}

The relational model in \code{database/schema.sql} was designed to satisfy both course constraints and collaboration workflows.
The design principles are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Normalized ownership structure}: users, projects, membership, join requests, and tasks are separated by concern.
    \item \textbf{Explicit role modeling}: owner/member roles are represented in \code{project\_members}.
    \item \textbf{Controlled lifecycle behavior}: foreign-key actions enforce cascade deletion or nullable assignment where needed.
    \item \textbf{Query-friendly indexing}: key lookup fields (code, owner, status, due date) are indexed.
\end{itemize}

The schema supports all required CRUD and reporting actions while preserving referential consistency under concurrent team usage.
